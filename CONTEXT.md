# Kian Academy

A personal learning platform organized around external resources, preparation, practice, and reflection.

## Language

**Resource**:
A particular external book, article, lecture series, or other work to study. Each resource is its own unit, with study, practice, and reflection activities.
_Avoid_: Lesson, module, subject unit

**Tier**:
One of five levels describing a resource's intended audience and assumed knowledge. Tier is independent of completion and confidence.

**Entry point (Tier 1)**:
A resource designed for a general reader with ordinary working knowledge of the world, no prior study, and a manageable scope for independent learning. A university course or textbook does not qualify merely because it is introductory.

**Foundations (Tier 2)**:
A resource that builds on entry-level familiarity with its topic.

**Intermediate (Tier 3)**:
A resource that assumes established foundations and broader relevant reading.

**Advanced (Tier 4)**:
A resource that assumes substantial knowledge of a subject and its methods.

**Specialist (Tier 5)**:
A resource that assumes expertise in a narrow area, beyond what a general practitioner of its broader discipline would usually know.

**Tag**:
A flexible, overlapping description of an aspect of a resource. Tags express interests and overlap, not exclusive subject categories.

**Specific prerequisite**:
A named resource that must be completed before another resource can be started. A resource above Tier 1 needs at least one specific prerequisite from the immediately preceding tier; all its named prerequisites must come from that tier.

**Volume prerequisite**:
A resource-specific minimum count of completed resources at a particular lower tier, matching any of a chosen set of tags. A resource counts once within a requirement; separate requirements must each be satisfied, and named prerequisites can also contribute to counts.

**Ready**:
A resource whose specific and volume prerequisites are all met. Entry points are ready without prior study.

**Needs prerequisite setup**:
A planned resource above Tier 1 that does not yet have a suitable named prerequisite. It remains visible for planning without being offered as ready to study.

**Completion**:
A record that external study, a saved practice response, and a self-assessment were finished. Completion does not certify mastery.

**Confidence**:
The learner's own assessment of their understanding, independent of completion.
